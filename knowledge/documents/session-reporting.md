# Session reporting and responsible language

A SmartContour report should include patient identifiers used by the project (name, id, age, notes), session times, device id, pressure summary, hotspot zone, recommendations, and any AI explanation with retrieved sources.

Language rules for the assistant and reports:
- Describe pressure distribution and relative hotspots.
- Recommend reassessment after contouring.
- State that guidance is prototype fitting support.
- Do not claim a medical diagnosis, ulcer risk score, or guaranteed clinical outcome.
- If the knowledge base has no matching passage, say that evidence was not found.

PDF reports are digital records of a fitting session for the academic project, not hospital medical records.
