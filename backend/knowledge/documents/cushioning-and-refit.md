# Soft Insert Contouring and Refit Guidance

This SmartContour knowledge document describes prototype cushioning recommendations generated when a zone is HIGH.

Recommended language: consider additional soft cushioning in the corresponding socket region and reassess pressure after fitting. Do not present this as a diagnosis of skin injury, vascular compromise, or socket failure.

Typical reasons Zone 1 (anterior-medial / tibial crest analog) may read high:
- socket is locally tight over the anterior residual limb
- insufficient relief or padding over a bony ridge
- sleeve sensor sitting on a firm edge
- volume change of the residual limb during the session

Typical reasons Zone 2 (anterior-lateral / fibular head analog) may read high:
- inadequate relief near the fibular head analog
- lateral socket wall too aggressive
- sensor stacked against a trim line

After adding a soft insert or locally contouring a pad:
1. Confirm the FSR has not shifted.
2. Start a new fitting session or clearly mark the refit in notes.
3. Watch whether the hotspot magnitude falls and whether load redistributes to neighboring zones.
4. Treat sudden drops to unavailable as a wiring or contact issue, not as clinical improvement.

Recommendations are stored with session id, zone, current/peak/average values, and a disclaimer. They support digital reports and the RAG assistant. They must not be mixed with Demo Mode simulated numbers.
