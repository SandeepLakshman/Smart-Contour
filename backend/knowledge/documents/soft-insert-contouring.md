# Soft insert contouring guidance for prototype sockets

Soft inserts (liners, pads, or locally contoured cushioning) are commonly used to improve comfort where a socket contacts a bony prominence or a high-load patch of residual-limb tissue.

When a hotspot is observed:

1. Identify the zone with the highest sustained or peak pressure.
2. Confirm it is not a brief transient from a single step unless it repeats.
3. Consider additional soft cushioning in the corresponding socket region.
4. Recheck pressure after the fitting change, using the same device placement.

If pressure remains high after cushioning, the issue may be alignment, socket volume, or sensor placement rather than padding thickness alone.

Do not interpret a HIGH classification as tissue injury. It is a relative alert from the SmartContour prototype.
